Frequently Asked Questions

Will my income data be stored on the blockchain?

Absolutely not. Your income data is kept as a private witness in your browser's memory. Only a cryptographic proof (True/False verification) is sent to the blockchain.

Who can see my application data?

No one. The foundation only sees whether you are eligible based on the math of the ZK proof. They never see your raw financial figures.

What wallet do I need?

You need a Midnight-compatible wallet, such as the Lace Wallet, configured for the Midnight Testnet.
