Frequently Asked Questions

Will my income data be stored on the blockchain?

No. Your income is a private witness: it is used on your machine to build the proof and is never written to the chain. The chain only receives the proof, the yes/no result and an anonymous application ID.

Who can see my application data?

Anyone can see that an application was made, its yes/no result and its anonymous application ID. Nobody, including the foundation, can see your income or tell which person or wallet the application belongs to.

What wallet do I need?

The Lace wallet on Midnight Preprod, with tDUST for fees (press "Generate tDUST" in Lace) and a local proof server at localhost:6300 selected under Settings » Midnight. The README explains the setup.
