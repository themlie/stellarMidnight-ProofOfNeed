How it Works

ProofOfNeed uses Midnight's zero-knowledge technology to verify eligibility without ever seeing your sensitive data. Here is the step-by-step process:

1. Input Income Locally

You connect Lace and enter your family's income. It is kept as a private witness in the page's memory and never sent to a server, the foundation or the chain.

2. Generate ZK Proof

The contract's check_eligibility circuit runs on your machine, and the prover Lace uses (a local proof server) turns the result into a zero-knowledge proof that your income is below the threshold.

3. Verified on Midnight

The network verifies the proof. The public ledger records a yes/no answer and an anonymous application ID that blocks a second application, and never the income. Paying stipends to eligible students is planned for a later version.
