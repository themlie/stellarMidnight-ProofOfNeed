How it Works

MidScholarship uses Midnight's zero-knowledge technology to verify eligibility without ever seeing your sensitive data. Here is the step-by-step process:

1. Input Income Locally

You enter your family's income details into the browser. This data stays completely local and never leaves your device.

2. Generate ZK Proof

The Midnight smart contract circuit runs a verification locally. It generates a cryptographic proof that you meet the criteria, without revealing the actual numbers.

3. Trustless Approval

The foundation receives the proof and issues the scholarship. They can cryptographically verify you are eligible, but your privacy remains 100% intact.
